## TL;DR — The Decision

**Launch NOW (fastest + cheapest):** Get **ANCC nursing contact hours through a state nurses association Approver Unit** — specifically **Montana Nurses Association** at a published, flat **$500** for an 8–16 contact-hour enduring activity. Turnaround is a few weeks. This covers your **RN and NP** audience directly (AANP grants reciprocity to ANCC contact hours — see the big note below), which is the majority of your buyers.

**If you also want physicians (MDs/DOs) and PAs covered in the same launch:** Add a **joint providership** with a Joint-Accredited provider so you can also grant **AMA PRA Category 1 Credit™** (which PAs accept via NCCPA and physicians require). **Long-term TMAA play:** Do **NOT** try to become an ANCC or Joint-Accredited provider yet. Both require a **12–18 month operating track record of already-delivered CE** before you can even apply, plus **$5k–$10k+/year** in fees. Instead, run 12–18 months of activities *through a joint provider under the TMAA brand*, build the required track record and file history, then apply for TMAA's own ANCC provider unit (or Joint Accreditation) as the monetization engine for the 31k-member audience.

## CRITICAL SIMPLIFYING FACT — NPs don't need a separate accreditation

**The AANP recognizes ACCME and ANCC as approved accreditors and grants reciprocity for AANPCP credit.** In plain terms: **an activity accredited for ANCC nursing contact hours (or AMA PRA Category 1 Credit) already counts for nurse practitioners** for their AANPCB recertification and most state NP license renewals.

This means you usually do **NOT** need to pay for a separate AANP accreditation. It collapses your problem:
- **RNs + NPs** → covered by **ANCC contact hours** (cheapest route: state nurses association approver).
- **MDs/DOs** → need **AMA PRA Category 1 Credit™** (ACCME line).
- **PAs** → accept **AMA PRA Category 1 Credit™** (NCCPA reciprocity).

So the real question is just: *do you need the physician/PA (AMA PRA Cat 1) line, or is nurse/NP contact hours enough for launch?* For an aesthetics/weight-loss provider audience that skews NP/RN, **ANCC contact hours alone is a legitimate, defensible launch.**
*Source: AANP reciprocity confirmed via Amedco accreditation statement and Medical Education Resources FAQ (URLs below).*

## ROUTE 1 — Joint Providership with an ACCME / Joint-Accredited Provider

**What it is:** A non-accredited entity (you/TMAA) partners with an already-accredited provider. You build and deliver the content; they lend their accreditation, review your activity against the Standards for Integrity & Independence, and issue the credit. **This is the standard path for a course creator who wants physician (AMA PRA Cat 1) credit or all-professions IPCE credit without becoming accredited themselves.**

**Joint Accreditation (IPCE)** is the gold standard: one review covers **physicians, nurses, NPs, PAs, pharmacists** (and often psychologists, dietitians, SWs, dentists, athletic trainers) under a single "IPCE credit" mark. Flag below shows which vendors offer full Joint Accreditation.

**Typical joint-providership cost reality (industry ranges, since most require quotes):** For a single enduring/on-demand activity, expect roughly **$3,000–$12,000+** depending on credit types, number of hours, whether they host it on their LMS, and whether commercial support is involved. Full IPCE all-professions activities sit at the higher end. **Only PAMED publishes a rate; everyone else is a direct quote request.**

**Credit types granted:** AMA PRA Category 1 Credit™ (MD/DO + PAs via NCCPA), ANCC contact hours (RN/NP), and with full Joint Accreditation also ACPE (pharmacy) and the unified IPCE credit.

**Turnaround:** 4–12 weeks typical from signed agreement to launch (must be reviewed and approved BEFORE the activity is available — no retroactive credit).

**Application requirements (all joint providers):** professional-practice **gap/needs assessment**, **measurable learning objectives**, **faculty/planner disclosures** (financial relationships with ineligible companies + mitigation), **content validation** (evidence-based, no commercial bias), **post-test** (typically ≥70–75% to pass for enduring material), **learner evaluation** form, and **certificate/credit reporting**.

**Renewal/expiration:** Enduring materials are typically certified for **up to 3 years**, with a required content review at least once during the term. Each new activity = new application/fee.

## ROUTE 2 — ANCC Nursing Contact Hours via a State Nurses Association Approver Unit  ⭐ CHEAPEST LEGIT ROUTE

**What it is:** ANCC-accredited **Approver Units** (mostly state nurses associations) will review and approve a *single* educational activity for ANCC nursing contact hours without you becoming an accredited provider yourself. This is the standard low-cost path for a nurse-majority audience — and because **AANP honors ANCC credit, it covers your NPs too.**

### Montana Nurses Association (MNA) — PUBLISHED, flat, cheap
Individual Activity Approval fee schedule (effective Jan 1, 2023, still current):
| Contact hours | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 4 | $150 |
| **>4–8** | **$300** |
| **>8–16** | **$500** ← your 8–12 hr course lands here |
| >16–30 | $600 |
| >30 | Contact MNA |

- **20% discount** if the nurse planner is an active MNA member.
- Late fee (<20 business days out): +$200. Expedite (≤5 business days): +$300. Optional priority review: +$300.
- **Approval term: 2 years for enduring materials.**
- No retroactive credit. Fee invoiced on receipt; doesn't guarantee approval.
- Contact: jennifer@mtnurses.org / mtnurses.smapply.org

### Ohio Nurses Association (ONA) — comparable route, quote/portal
- ANCC-accredited approver (OBN-001-91). Individual activities approved for a **2-year** term.
- Application only via ONA's software; must submit **≥6 weeks** before activity or pay expedite; **<2 weeks out = not accepted.**
- Fee amounts not published on the FAQ page — request via ce.ohnurses.org. Historically in the same **$500–$2,000** band depending on hours; MNA is cheaper and fully published, so lead with MNA.

### Other legit approver units to price-shop
Wisconsin Nurses Association, Pennsylvania State Nurses Association (PSNA), and most state nurses associations run the same individual-activity approver program. All follow the same ANCC Approver criteria; fees vary $150–$2k by hours.

**Credit types granted:** ANCC nursing contact hours (valid for RN and NP renewal; AANP reciprocity covers NP recert).
**Requirements:** Named **qualified Nurse Planner** on the planning committee (this is the one structural must — you as an FNP can serve, or MNA can advise), **gap/needs assessment**, **learning outcomes**, **planner/faculty disclosures + conflict mitigation**, **post-test**, **evaluation**, attendance/completion records.
**Turnaround:** A few weeks; submit ≥20 business days out at MNA to avoid the late fee.
**Renewal/expiration:** **2 years**, then re-apply (with content refresh).

> ⚠️ **US-only caveat on the "direct ANCC" route:** ANCC's own *NCPD Individual Activity Accreditation* is **restricted to organizations located OUTSIDE the United States.** As a US entity you cannot use ANCC directly for a single activity — you must go through a US **Approver Unit** (Montana/Ohio/etc.). Don't waste time emailing ANCC directly for this.

## ROUTE 3 — AANP Approved Provider / AANP Individual Activity (NP-specific)

**What it is:** Direct AANP accreditation of an NP CE activity, or becoming an AANP Approved Provider.

**Verdict: Usually unnecessary and partly closed.**
1. **Redundant for most needs** — AANP already accepts ANCC and ACCME credit by reciprocity, so Route 1 or Route 2 covers NPs.
2. **AANP Approved Provider status is now invitation-only** — as of **Jan 5, 2026**, AANP accepts new Approved Provider applications **by invitation only.** Not an open door for a new course creator.
3. **AANP Individual Activity accreditation** is still open: apply via aanpedu.smapply.org. Requirements: NP org number or IRS tax ID, certificate of completion, evaluation, agenda, marketing, planner/faculty names+emails, session objectives, and **pharmacology objectives + minutes** (valuable — GLP-1 dosing/titration content can earn **pharmacology hours**, which NPs specifically need). Submit **≤90 days** before the event; ≤14 business days = expedited.
   - **Fees: NOT published on the site** — they live in the 2026 AANP Accreditation Policy Handbook fee schedule (PDF wouldn't render for extraction). NPO members get "a substantial discount." **Direct quote / handbook lookup required.**

**When to bother with AANP directly:** Only if you want the explicit **AANP-branded pharmacology hours** as a marketing differentiator to NPs. Otherwise skip — it's the weakest ROI of the four routes for your case.

## ROUTE 4 — TMAA Becomes Its Own Accredited Provider (Long-Term Monetization)

**The prize:** If TMAA holds its own ANCC provider unit or Joint Accreditation, it can accredit **unlimited** activities in-house at marginal cost and **sell CE to its ~31k-member aesthetics audience** as a recurring revenue line. This is the real long game.

**The blocker: you can't apply cold.** Both credentials require a **pre-existing track record of already-delivered CE** before you're even eligible.

### Option 4A — ANCC NCPD Accredited Provider (nurse/NP CE at scale)
- **Application fee (from Jan 1, 2026): $4,950 new applicant** / $2,150 reaccreditation.
- **Annual fee:** ~$2,000–$3,100/yr depending on org type. As a professional association, TMAA would likely fall under **C/SNA (~$2,000)** or **OA (~$2,300)** — cheapest annual tiers.
- **Expedited cycle:** +$1,500. Plus assorted operational fees (deferral $1,500, progress report $750, etc.).
- **Timeline:** Must **implement the ANCC NCPD criteria and run activities for ≥6 months BEFORE applying.** Application cycles open 3x/yr (Mar 1 / Jul 1 / Nov 1). Appraisal takes **2–6 months.** First accreditation term = up to **2 years** (then up to 4).
- **Realistic all-in first-year cost: ~$7,000–$9,000+** and **~9–15 months** door-to-door.
- **Requirement:** A Primary Nurse Planner + functioning NCPD unit structure, sample activity files proving the process.

### Option 4B — Joint Accreditation (IPCE — the all-professions engine)
- **Eligibility (hard gate):** Fully functional interprofessional CE structure **for the past 18 months**, AND **≥25% of your activities over those 18 months must be interprofessional** (planned by/for 2+ professions), AND meet **100% of the 12 Joint Accreditation criteria.**
- **Fees:** Not published — quote from Joint Accreditation staff. Real-world: **application + annual fees generally run higher than ANCC alone**, commonly **$10k+/year** all-in when you factor staff time.
- **Timeline:** Realistically **18+ months** just to become *eligible*, plus the review cycle.

### The TMAA playbook (sequence it right)
1. **Months 0–2:** Launch the GLP-1 course NOW via **Route 2 (MNA, $500)** and/or a **joint provider (Route 1)** — but run everything **under the TMAA brand** so the activity files, planner records, and evaluations accrue to TMAA.
2. **Months 0–18:** Deliberately build a **portfolio of ≥4–6 CE activities**, and make **≥25% interprofessional** (co-plan with a physician + pharmacist + NP) if you're aiming at Joint Accreditation. Keep clean files: gap analyses, disclosures, outcomes data, evaluations.
3. **Month ~6–9:** If nurse/NP CE is the main monetization target, begin **ANCC NCPD provider** implementation (6-month self-run requirement), then apply in the next cycle.
4. **Month ~18+:** If the all-professions market is proven, apply for **Joint Accreditation** using the 18-month interprofessional track record you built.
5. **Monetize:** Once accredited, sell CE-bearing courses/memberships to the 31k audience; CE is a well-known retention and dues-justification lever for associations.

## STEP-BY-STEP SETUP CHECKLIST — Recommended Route (MNA, launch now)

**1. Confirm the Nurse Planner.** MNA requires a qualified Nurse Planner on the planning committee. Derek (FNP) likely qualifies; confirm with MNA (jennifer@mtnurses.org) or recruit an RN/NP with NCPD experience.

**2. Pin down contact hours.** Time the 11 modules + expected reading of the 4 reference docs. ANCC contact hours = 60 min = 1.0 CH, in 15-min increments. Document the time-calculation method (either a pilot-tester timing or the 60-min rule). Your 8–12 hr estimate lands in the **$500** tier.

**3. Prepare these documents from existing course materials:**
   - **Gap / Needs Assessment** — the professional practice gap (e.g., "prescribers lack structured GLP-1 titration, side-effect, and obesity-management competency"), with evidence (guidelines, prescribing-trend data). *You can adapt marketing/positioning docs you already have.*
   - **Learning Outcomes** — 4–8 measurable objectives (Bloom's verbs), one set per module or overall. *Pull from module intros.*
   - **Content outline / agenda** — module list, durations, faculty per module.
   - **Faculty & Planner CVs/bios + Disclosure forms** — every planner and presenter discloses financial relationships with ineligible companies (pharma/device); document mitigation if any exist. (You'll disclose anything re: GLP-1 manufacturers, compounding pharmacies, etc.)
   - **Evidence/reference list** — the 4 reference docs + citations proving content is current and non-promotional (no brand bias).
   - **Post-test** — question bank mapped to objectives; enduring materials typically require **≥75% pass**, unlimited or limited retakes defined.
   - **Learner evaluation form** — MNA usually provides a template; captures objective achievement, bias, and practice-change intent.
   - **Completion certificate template** — with the exact ANCC accreditation statement MNA gives you (do not write your own credit statement).

**4. Register on MNA's portal** (mtnurses.smapply.org), start the Individual Activity Application. Submit **≥20 business days** before you want it live to avoid the $200 late fee.

**5. Pay the $500** (apply the 20% member discount if your Nurse Planner is an MNA member → $400). Invoice issued on receipt.

**6. Revise per reviewer feedback,** get the approval letter + official credit statement, then embed the statement + certificate logic into your LMS/course platform.

**7. Set a 2-year calendar reminder** to re-apply/refresh content before expiration.

**8. (Parallel, optional)** Request AMA PRA Cat 1 quotes from PAMED + 2 Joint-Accredited vendors if you decide to add physician/PA credit. If yes, that activity is reviewed separately — don't let it block the nurse-CH launch.

**9. Brand everything TMAA** from day one (planner-of-record, files, evaluations) to seed the 18-month track record for Route 4.
